What counts as an electrical emergency?

Sparking outlets, burning smells, sudden total power loss, exposed or damaged wiring, or a safety switch that keeps tripping. Any of these means stop using the area and call us. If there is an immediate risk to life or fire, call 000 first.

How quickly can you respond to an emergency callout?

Emergency callouts get priority scheduling ahead of routine work. Call us directly on 0438 883 273 and we'll talk you through immediate safety steps while arranging someone to get to you.

Is a tripping safety switch actually an emergency?

A safety switch that won't stay on, or trips repeatedly, is doing exactly what it's designed to do: flagging a real fault. It's not something to keep resetting and ignoring. Get it looked at.
