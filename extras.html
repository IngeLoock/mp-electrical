EV charger, 7kW single-phase

Home EV charging point, standard single-phase install.

EV charger, 22kW three-phase

Faster charging. Requires three-phase power (pair with the three-phase upgrade if needed).

Three-phase power upgrade

Upgrades single-phase homes to three-phase, often required for larger batteries, EV chargers or off-grid systems.

Battery expansion module

Adds extra kWh of storage to an existing battery package without installing a second system.

Backup generator (petrol or diesel)

An additional resilience layer for off-grid style setups and outage-prone areas.

Solar hot water diverter

Redirects excess solar to your hot water system instead of exporting it, maximising self-consumption.

Whole-home energy monitoring

Circuit-level usage monitoring beyond the standard app monitoring included in every package.

Export limiting device

Required by some Energex connection agreements. Caps export to the grid at an approved limit.

Whole-home surge protection

Surge protector fitted at the switchboard.
