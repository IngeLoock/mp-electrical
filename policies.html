Privacy policy

We collect your name, phone number, email and job details only to quote and carry out electrical work you've asked about. We don't sell or share your details with third parties, other than suppliers and subcontractors needed to complete your job, and we keep records as required by Queensland electrical safety and tax law. You can ask us to update or delete your details at any time on 0438 883 273.

Booking and cancellation policy

  • Quotes are free and in writing. Work starts only once you've accepted the quote.
  • Please give at least 24 hours' notice to reschedule or cancel a booked job. Late cancellations may incur a call-out fee where we've already travelled or ordered materials.
  • Solar and battery installs may require a deposit on acceptance, shown clearly on your quote.

Service area and call-out terms

  • We service greater Brisbane, Ipswich, Logan and the Gold Coast. Jobs outside this area are quoted case by case.
  • Emergency callouts are prioritised ahead of routine work. Call-out fees, where they apply, are told to you before we attend.
  • If there is an immediate risk to life or fire, call 000 first.

Terms of service

  • All work is carried out by licensed electricians to Queensland electrical safety standards, with a certificate of compliance issued where required.
  • Package prices are indicative and confirmed on a free on-site assessment. Final pricing is in your written quote.
  • Manufacturer warranties apply to supplied products. Workmanship is warranted as stated on your quote, with an extended option available.
  • Payment terms are shown on your invoice.
