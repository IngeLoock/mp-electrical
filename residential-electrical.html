Why it matters

Older homes, newer standards.

A lot of Brisbane's housing stock, especially classic Queenslanders, was wired to standards that have moved on considerably. If your switchboard still has ceramic fuses instead of circuit breakers, or you're not sure whether you have a safety switch, it's worth a check.

We'll tell you plainly if something needs attention now versus something you can plan for later. No scare tactics, just a straight assessment.

How do I know if I need a switchboard upgrade?

Signs include ceramic fuses instead of circuit breakers, frequent tripped fuses, an old or discoloured switchboard, or planning solar or EV charging additions. A free assessment will confirm.

Can you install a fan I already bought?

Yes. We're happy to install customer-supplied fans and fixtures, or supply and install one for you.

Do you work on rental properties?

Yes. We regularly work with landlords, property managers and tenants for repairs and compliance work.
