Not sure which tier?

A package is a starting point, not the final word.

Every household's roof, usage and budget are different. We look at your roof size and orientation, your average daily usage from recent bills, whether you're home during the day or evening, and future plans like an EV or bigger appliances. That decides panel count and battery size, not just picking the biggest package.
