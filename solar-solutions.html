Why battery storage

Worth it, if it's sized right.

A well matched battery means you use more of the solar you already generate, instead of exporting it for a fraction of its value and buying it back at night.

Lower bills

Store excess daytime solar and use it in the evening instead of buying it back from the grid.

Outage backup

Selected setups keep key circuits running when the grid goes down. We tell you exactly what yours covers.

Future-proofing

A properly upgraded switchboard means your home is ready for EV charging and further solar expansion.

FAQ

Solar and battery questions.

Anything else, give us a call on 0438 883 273.

How much does solar or a battery cost in Brisbane?

It depends on your roof, household usage and the system size that actually suits your home. Our solar packages start from $6,109 and solar plus battery from $11,468, but we don't quote sight unseen. Every job gets a free on-site assessment and a written quote before anything is locked in.

Will a battery keep my power on during an outage?

It depends how the system is set up. Some configurations back up essential circuits during an outage; others are sized purely for evening savings with no backup at all. We'll be upfront about exactly what a given setup does and doesn't cover.

Do I need existing solar to add a battery?

No. We install solar plus battery systems from scratch, and we also retrofit batteries onto homes that already have solar installed.

How long does a solar or battery installation take?

Most residential installs are done in a day, depending on system size and whether switchboard work is needed first. You get a clear timeframe with your quote.
